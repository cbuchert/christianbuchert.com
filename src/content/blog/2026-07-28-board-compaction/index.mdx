## The key insight: hole-free + local ⟹ the corner staircase

{/* TODO: the heart of the story. Two properties you'd want — hole-free (every
    row/col contiguous from an edge) and move-local (each line anchored to a
    fixed edge). Together they ARE the staircase. The only free variable is which
    corner, which merely relocates the long row/column — never removes it.
    Corollaries: center-anchoring breaks locality (append shifts ~half a line);
    to shorten the left column you must stop left-anchoring → holes. You cannot
    de-lopside at the packing layer without paying in holes or teleports. */}
